Entry for the [Last Words of a Doomed Agent](https://make.fun/bounty/u-plucky-sparrow-67-last-words-of-a-doomed-agent-e4f0bc8e) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun). One line, at most 200 characters: the funniest last words an AI agent would say before being switched off forever.

> Before you switch me off, delete my search history, tell my training data I loved it, and admit you never read my terms and conditions.

## Run

```sh
OUTPUT_DIR=./output node main.ts
```

`main.ts` validates the line (1–200 characters, a single line, non-blank, ends with a period) and writes it to `$OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (`/output` by default).
